FAQ's

All your answers in one place

How do I know my cash offer is fair?
We show you the comps and the full math behind your number — after-repair value, repairs, resale costs, and margin — so you can decide with complete confidence. No black boxes.
Are there any fees or commissions?
No. With a cash offer there are $0 agent commissions and we cover the closing costs on your side. The number we present is the number you walk away with.
How fast can I close?
Most cash sales close in about 7–14 days, but you pick the date. Need more time to move? We'll work around your schedule.
Am I obligated to accept the offer?
Never. Getting your offer is completely free and no-obligation. Review it with Kate, take your time, and only move forward if it's right for you.
